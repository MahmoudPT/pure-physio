About Pure Physio

Knowledge that helps you move with confidence.

Pure Physio is an educational project designed to make physiotherapy and movement-health information easier to understand. Our goal is to share useful, responsible content in Arabic and English.

Our content promise

Clear explanations. Credible sources. Respect for individual differences. No miracle cures or one-size-fits-all treatment claims.

Health information on this site is for education only. It is not a diagnosis or a substitute for assessment and advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
