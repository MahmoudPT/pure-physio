About Me

I am Mahmoud Ayman, a fourth-year physiotherapy student at Kafrelsheikh University. I am interested in physiotherapy and physical rehabilitation, and I am continuously working to develop my scientific and practical knowledge.

I created Pure Physio as an educational platform dedicated to simplifying medical information and raising awareness about musculoskeletal conditions, physiotherapy approaches, and rehabilitation.
